## Artist statement (español)

Trabajo desde el gesto. Muchas de mis piezas empiezan sin una idea fija: la obra se va armando mientras la hago, y el movimiento de la mano decide tanto como yo. Otras veces parto de un concepto y lo construyo poco a poco, o dejo que todo sea gesto. En los tres casos busco lo mismo: que el caos y el sentimiento queden en la superficie.

Llevo diez años haciendo arte. Empecé editando fotos y haciendo collage, tuve mi propia marca de ropa, hice diseño gráfico, graffiti y stickers, y de ahí llegué a la pintura. Hoy trabajo con tinta, acrílico, aerosol y carboncillo, y también con medias y mallas. Escojo estos materiales porque se dejan manipular de muchas maneras y responden rápido a la mano.

Casi toda mi obra es negro sobre blanco, los colores con los que me identifico. Cuando una pieza necesita decir algo más fuerte, entra el rojo o el rosa.

Quiero que quien mire mi obra sienta algo que no sentía hace mucho tiempo: añoranza, nostalgia, ese tipo de sentimiento.

## Artist statement (English)

My work starts with gesture. Many of my pieces begin without a fixed idea: the work takes shape as I make it, and the movement of my hand decides as much as I do. Other times I start from a concept and build it slowly, or let the whole piece be gesture. Either way, I am after the same thing: leaving chaos and feeling on the surface.

I have been making art for ten years. I started editing photos and making collage, ran my own clothing brand, did graphic design, graffiti and stickers, and from there I came to painting. Today I work with ink, acrylic, spray paint and charcoal, and also with stockings and fishnets. I choose these materials because they can be handled in many ways and respond quickly to the hand.

Almost all my work is black on white, the colors I identify with. When a piece needs to say something louder, red or pink comes in.

I want people who look at my work to feel something they haven't felt in a long time: longing, nostalgia, that kind of feeling.

## Long bio (~150 words)

Patricia [Last name] was born in Caguas, Puerto Rico, where she lives and works. She has been making art for ten years across many media: she started out editing photos and making collage, ran her own clothing brand, and did graphic design, graffiti, stickers and patterns before coming to painting. She has also experimented with printmaking, papermaking and metal sculpture.

Self-taught at first, she is now formalizing her studies at [university, program, year].

Her work is gestural abstraction in ink, acrylic, spray paint and charcoal, sometimes with materials such as stockings and fishnets. She works mostly in black on white and uses red and pink as a pop of color. Some pieces are planned and some come straight from the gesture; either way she is after chaos, feeling and a nostalgia the viewer can recognize.
