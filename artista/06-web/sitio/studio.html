I'm Patricia, a visual artist from Caguas, Puerto Rico. My pieces start without a fixed plan: the work builds itself while my hand moves, and the gesture decides as much as I do.

I've been making art for about ten years: photo collage, my own clothing brand, graphic design, graffiti and stickers, and now painting. Almost everything begins in black on white. Red and fuchsia come in when a piece needs to say something louder.

I want you to feel something you haven't felt in a long time.
