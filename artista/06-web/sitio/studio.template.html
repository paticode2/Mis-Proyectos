hello,

I'm Patricia, and I work from the gesture.

I'm a visual artist from Caguas, Puerto Rico. My pieces start without a fixed plan: the work builds itself while my hand moves, and the gesture decides as much as I do.

Almost everything begins in black on white. Red and fuchsia come in when a piece needs to say something louder.
